# Third-party notices

## Feesh — Fishing quality-of-life behavior

This mod includes Forge 1.8.9 behavior ports inspired by Sleepy-Panda's [Feesh](https://github.com/Sleepy-Panda/Feesh), including tracked-catch efficiency, Great White Festival personal-best, and optional rare-creature highlighting/alerts. These are implemented using this mod's own local message and entity-name parsing; the old Feesh-style Hook Timer and Bite Alert behavior are not included.

## Not Enough Fakepixel — M7 dragon areas and Fakepixel fishing features

M7 relic/dragon-area geometry and the final-phase cue are adapted from Not Enough Fakepixel 1.2.4's [`DragonCloseAlert.java`](https://github.com/im2pb/NotEnoughFakepixel/blob/1.2.4/src/main/java/com/nef/notenoughfakepixel/features/skyblock/dungeons/DragonCloseAlert.java) and [`M7RelicWaypoints.java`](https://github.com/im2pb/NotEnoughFakepixel/blob/1.2.4/src/main/java/com/nef/notenoughfakepixel/features/skyblock/dungeons/M7RelicWaypoints.java). A persisted setting defaults to keeping the area boxes visible through dragon spawns; if turned off, they disappear after the first dragon appears.

The Fakepixel Catch Notifications settings and Legendary Sea Creature/Trophy Fish notifier behavior are adapted from [Not Enough Fakepixel](https://github.com/im2pb/NotEnoughFakepixel). This mod does not bundle or depend on the upstream Fabric implementation.
