# RNG Carried Bald (Forge 1.8.9)

A client-side Forge mod for Hypixel SkyBlock with optional Fakepixel-only fishing enhancements. It provides independently movable Slayer and Fishing HUDs with individually adjustable translucent backgrounds. The Fishing category groups the mob counter, Legendary sea-creature and Trophy Fish notifiers, nearby sea-creature HP, rare-creature outlines/alerts, session efficiency, and a Great White Festival personal best. It also provides an F7/M7 world overlay for boss/dragon hitboxes plus M7 final-phase dragon areas, Totem of Corruption and SOS Flare countdowns plus timers for all four Wolf Slayer Power Orbs, a local Lord Jawbus alert, and automatic fishing/Slayer timers. The main box automatically switches its active history to the Slayer associated with each newly recognized drop, while keeping separate histories for **Spider, Revenant (Rev), Blaze, Wolf, and Enderman**. It does not show a transient item-drop popup; Slayer names and box numbers are not displayed. Drop rows show the stack count, item, and total from the saved per-item price (or “price not set”); rare-drop detail labels are hidden. The total profit line sums only the priced totals in the currently visible Slayer history. The separate Fishing box is independently positioned. The mod never sends chat or automates gameplay; the optional rare-creature alert plays a vanilla sound only when enabled. All custom HUDs/world outlines hide while you hold Tab for the player list, which stays the vanilla tab list. Feature switches are independent.

## Install

1. Use **Minecraft Java Edition 1.8.9 with Forge 11.15.1.2318**.
2. Put `RNG-Carried-Bald-1.0.51.jar` in `.minecraft/mods`.
3. Launch the Forge 1.8.9 profile.

## Tracker controls

- The `/trackergui` window heading reads **RNG carried Bald**. Type `/tg`, `/trackergui`, or `/spidertracker` to open the category-style settings window. Its categories are **Slayer, Fishing, Dungeon, Deployables, Appearance, Layout, and About**, grouped in the sidebar as **Tracking, World, Timers, HUD, and Info**. Fishing contains three panels: **Mob Counter, Catch Notifications, and Sea Creature HP**. Mob Counter holds the catch counter, efficiency, and Festival PB; Catch Notifications contains Fakepixel Legendary/Trophy notifiers plus the retained Rare Creature/Jawbus controls. Imported catch notifiers operate only on Fakepixel. In the full-screen layout editor a bright cyan outline with white corner handles marks the selected box; it follows dragging, and its corner handle resizes the box. Buttons share consistent hover styling and green ON-state accents. **Appearance** includes separate 1%-100% background-opacity sliders for the Slayer tracker, Fishing counter, Sea Creature HP, and Deployables panel (35% default); selecting a slider updates the preview. The **Layout** category opens a full-screen editor that shows all four HUD boxes together. Drag each box to move it, and drag its corner handle (right or down to grow, back to shrink) to resize between 1% and 100%; the corner readout follows the cursor while you drag. Positions and sizes save automatically; Slayer and Fishing share one size setting, and this editor is the only place panel sizes are set. All custom HUD boxes hide while the chat input is open, as they do while the player list is held.
- Use the **Slayer** category to turn the Slayer HUD on/off, type exact X/Y screen coordinates, or drag the live preview by its header. All mod HUD panels automatically hide while you hold **Tab** to view the player list, then return when released. The Tracker toggle controls only the Slayer HUD; it does not enable or disable Fishing.
- **Clear history** opens a chooser for Enderman, Wolf, Spider, Blaze, Revenant, or Fishing. Selecting one clears only that history. Clearing Fishing does not remove Slayer drops; clearing a Slayer does not remove Fishing counts. `/spidertracker clear` clears all five Slayer histories and the Fishing counters.
- Use **Fishing** → **Mob Counter** to independently enable the sea-creature counter and cast-to-reel timer. It controls the catch-count portion of the Fishing box; the Slayer HUD toggle does not control Fishing. The cast-to-reel timer starts when the bobber appears after casting and stops when it is reeled in; the most recent duration stays visible until the next cast. A separate `Splash -> pull` stopwatch starts on a splash sound near your bobber, counts up until the downward-pull detector fires, and holds the measured time until your next cast; its HUD row stays hidden until a splash is detected. Enable **Efficiency tracker** for a session count and tracked Legendary/Jawbus catches per hour; it starts on your first local cast (or first qualifying catch), resets when changed OFF/ON or when the world changes, and ignores untracked non-Legendary catches. Enable **Great White festival PB** for current-window and best Great White counts; the window ends on Hypixel's local Fishing Festival end line or after a 61-minute safety window from the first Great White announcement. No Nurse/Blue/Tiger Shark counts are included. The fishing box is independently movable: type its X/Y position or drag the Fishing preview header. By default, it sits beside the Slayer box when there is room, otherwise below it. The toggles, positions, independent panel opacity values, counts, and Great White personal best are saved in `.minecraft/config/spiderdropalert.properties`; session efficiency values are session-only.
- The **Fishing → Catch Notifications** panel has Fakepixel-only Legendary and Trophy Fish notifier toggles plus the retained Rare Creature and Jawbus controls. The Legendary notifier recognizes every tracked Legendary catch phrase and displays Sea Emperor aliases as The Loch Emperor; Trophy Fish notices accept Fakepixel system messages without a specific scoreboard sub-area. The Rare Creature alert plays a vanilla sound for recognized local tracked-creature announcements and excludes Jawbus. The previously added Fishing Alert countdown, ETA/INCOMING, Slug Mode, and `!!!` bite marker have been removed.
- Use **Deployables** for a separate, movable, resizable timer panel with its own adjustable background opacity. It has a master switch and independent timer switches for Totem of Corruption, SOS Flare, and the Power Orb group. The Power Orb group covers the four Wolf Slayer deployables: Radiant Power Orb (30s), Mana Flux Power Orb (30s), Overflux Power Orb (60s), and Plasmaflux Power Orb (60s), as listed on the [Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki Deployables page](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Deployables). Right-click starts the corresponding countdown; the Totem keeps its existing 2m fallback and syncs to its live in-world remaining-time label when detected, while SOS is 3m. Active rows can display together; each turns red in its last 10 seconds. Panel/group switches, position, and size are saved independently.
- In **Fishing → Sea Creature HP**, toggle a separate HUD for nearby Legendary/Mythic sea creatures; its background opacity is independent from the other panels. It reads nearby entity display/custom names and health (up to 8 entries within 64 blocks), sorted nearest first. The Legendary list includes all 12 current wiki entries: Water Hydra, The Loch Emperor (plus Sea Emperor name variants), Alligator, Abyssal Miner, Blue Ringed Octopus, Fiery Scuttler, Great White Shark, Phantom Fisher, Puddle Jumper, Silkbreeze, Thunder, and Yeti. Existing Mythic entries, including Jawbus and Reindrake, remain tracked. Name matching tolerates rarity or other nameplate text around the creature name. Drag its live preview or enter X/Y; resize it in the Layout editor, where a corner drag scales it anywhere from 1% to 100% in 1% steps. It only appears while a recognized creature is nearby, and its setting, position, and scale remain independent. The **Rare creature outlines** switch draws a visible-only box around those same supported nearby creatures, up to 64 blocks; it is independent from the HP panel and F7/M7 outline toggle.
- The **Fishing → Catch Notifications** panel has a separate **Jawbus title** toggle. When enabled, only your own local Hypixel chat line `You have angered a legendary creature... Lord Jawbus has arrived.` displays a centered `LORD JAWBUS` title. Party-member announcements and copied/player chat lines do not trigger it. It does not send chat or play a sound.
- Fishing counts only recognized catch-announcement messages for the wiki-**LEGENDARY** sea creatures: **The Loch Emperor, Water Hydra, Alligator, Abyssal Miner, Blue Ringed Octopus, Fiery Scuttler, Great White Shark, Phantom Fisher, Puddle Jumper, Silkbreeze, Thunder, and Yeti**. Legacy Sea Emperor catch lines beginning `The Sea Emperor`, `Sea Emperor`, or `A Sea Emperor` are counted under **The Loch Emperor**. **Lord Jawbus is also counted at your request** as an exception (the wiki rates him MYTHIC). Other fish/sea-creature messages are ignored. `Total creature` sums the tracked Legendary species plus Lord Jawbus.
- The Fishing box appears as soon as **Mob Counter** is ON; enabling either optional stats tracker also displays its rows in that box. With Mob Counter enabled, before a catch or cast it shows `Waiting for catches...`; while a bobber timer is running or held, it shows `Fishing timer - mm:ss`. Only species with at least one tracked catch are shown; zero-count species are hidden, and no `Total creature - 0` line is shown. For a Hydra-only catch, the row is `Water Hydra-1`, followed by the separator and `Total creature - 1`. With multiple species, the total sums only the tracked catches—for example, the illustrative total may be `Total creature - 54`.
- The Slayer box shows `Boss timer - mm:ss` from detection of a supported boss spawning until the Slayer quest-complete chat message. It covers Tarantula/Primordial Broodfather (Spider), Revenant/Atoned Horror, Inferno Demonlord, Sven Packmaster, and Voidgloom Seraph. The HUD timer itself never displays a Slayer name. Use **Appearance** to adjust the Slayer and Fishing HUD boxes from 70% to 150% (100% is the default) and to set independent background opacity for the Slayer, Fishing counter, Sea Creature HP, and Deployables panels. The Deployables panel has its own independent size controls. HUD toggles, positions, Fishing counts, and prices are saved locally.
- `/spidertracker toggle` toggles only the Slayer HUD from chat; it does not change the Fishing box state.

## F7/M7 rendering

- The **Dungeon** category has a master **F7/M7 hitboxes** switch plus independent switches for **Maxor, Storm, Goldor, Necron, each of the five dragons (Apex, Flame, Ice, Power, Soul), and the M7 dragon-area outlines**. Each dragon switch controls a moving hitbox outline on that dragon entity; the dragon-area switch controls the fixed relic-zone boxes. A separate **Persist after dragon spawn** switch controls whether M7 areas stay visible after the first dragon appears (ON by default; OFF hides them after that spawn). The world overlay recognizes standard **normal Floor VII/Master Mode Floor VII** sidebars and Fakepixel's `dung-#` dungeon sidebar when it lists a named M7 dragon health line. Dragon hitboxes use their matching statue color when the dragon name or spawn position identifies its type. The page reports whether a Floor 7 scoreboard is recognized. Boss and dragon nameplates search a wider area for their nearby entity; if the server sends only a nameplate, a same-color approximate fallback box is drawn instead of showing nothing. The overlay reuses entity scans for 250 ms between renders, and the sea-creature HP scan runs twice per second to reduce client-side frame work.
- During the M7 final-phase cue (`The Catacombs... are no more`) or Fakepixel's M7 dragon-health phase, the **M7 dragon areas** switch draws five NEF 1.2.4-style relic-area outlines (red, green, blue, orange, and purple). They remain visible while dragons spawn and through the rest of the M7 final phase; they reset when leaving M7 or unloading the world. This is M7-only; the separate F7/M7 boss and dragon hitboxes remain controlled by their own switches.
- Area positions, dimensions, and colors follow Not Enough Fakepixel 1.2.4's `DragonCloseAlert`; its final-phase chat cue follows `M7RelicWaypoints`. These coordinates have **not** been independently validated in a live Hypixel session.

## Quick Note (/qn)

Type `/qn` (alias `/quicknote`) to open a note pad without leaving the game. Every page has a **Heading**
you can click and type into - Enter or Tab drops you from the heading into the note itself - and the
heading is saved with the page. The pad opens on the last page with the cursor ready at the end,
pages turn with `Prev` / `Next`, and the **+ New page** button in the top-right corner adds one up to 99, and `Delete` empties a page (the last remaining page is cleared rather than removed, so there is
always somewhere to type). Enter starts a new line, Backspace and Delete edit, the arrow keys and
clicking move the caret, and the mouse wheel scrolls a long page. Every edit saves itself about
seven tenths of a second after you stop typing - and again when you press Esc, Done or swap pages -
so nothing is ever lost. Notes live in `config/spiderdropalert-notes.txt` as plain UTF-8 text, one
block per page separated by a `%%PAGE%%` marker line with the page's heading on a
`%%TITLE%% <heading>` line above it, so they can be read or edited outside Minecraft. Note lines
that would look like a marker are escaped with an extra `%`. The **Quality of Life -> Quick Note** page lists how many pages are stored
with their headings, opens the pad, and clears every page behind a confirmation.

## Dragon kill alert (M7)

During a Floor VII / M7 run the mod calls out the dragon worth killing: a dragon that is within **20
blocks of you** *and* within **15 blocks of its own statue** shows a big centred title in that
dragon's colour - `KILL POWER DRAGON`, `KILL APEX DRAGON`, `KILL SOUL DRAGON`, `KILL ICE DRAGON` or
`KILL FLAME DRAGON` - matching its area outline. Detection uses the NotEnoughFakepixel statue
anchors (Power `27,14,59`, Apex `27,14,94`, Soul `56,14,125`, Ice `84,14,94`, Flame `85,14,56`): a
dragon with a readable name matches by name, and an unnamed dragon entity falls back to the statue
it is parked at. If two dragons qualify, the one nearer you is named. The title holds for a moment
after the last sighting so it cannot flicker when you walk along the range boundary, and the check
only runs while the F7/M7 scoreboard is up. Toggle it in **Dungeons -> Dragon Areas -> Kill alert**.

## Manual item prices

Hold the item and use `/priceset <price>` or `/setprice <price>` to set its price; both commands detect the held item. Both also support typed-item syntax, such as `/setprice Tarantula Talisman 110k`. Use either command without arguments to check the detected held item. Prices can use `k`, `m`, or `b` suffixes; commas are optional. A number without a suffix is a literal coin amount: `/setprice 110` means 110 coins, while `/setprice 110k` means 110,000 coins. The command reports the result in chat.

Examples: `/priceset` (check held item), `/setprice 110k` (set that held item's price), `/setprice 1.5m`, or `/setprice Tarantula Talisman 110k` (typed name).

Repeated drops of the same item stack into one row within that Slayer's history; their counts add together and the total value is recalculated. Rows show the count, item, and total in parentheses, for example `20x Tarantula Silk(38k)` for a 1.9k unit price. A stack of 64 items priced at 110k each is shown as `64x Item Name(7.04m)`. A thin horizontal line separates the drop rows from `Total profit = ...`, which sums the saved-price totals of currently visible rows; rows marked `price not set` are excluded. For example, a single `64x Toxic Arrow Poison(7.04m)` row contributes `Total profit = 7.04m`. Rare-drop detail labels are hidden for every item. Duplicate chat events are ignored. Decimal-comma suffixes such as `1,9k` are accepted. Prices are saved locally and matched drop names have Minecraft color codes stripped. Wrapped stack messages such as `(322) x(Toxic Arrow Poison)` are normalized to the clean `322x Toxic Arrow Poison` row format.

## Detection

The Slayer drop filter only accepts a recognized rare-drop label at the start of the unformatted chat line, then checks for a known reward marker. The boss timer recognizes supported Slayer boss entities as they enter the client world (with `[BOSS]` dialogue as a chat fallback) and stops on `SLAYER QUEST COMPLETE!`. Fishing uses recognized creature-announcement phrases for the twelve wiki-Legendary species plus the requested Lord Jawbus exception; non-Legendary catch messages and player lines with a sender prefix are ignored. The new Fishing efficiency/Festival features reuse only those same accepted local announcements; the festival statistic intentionally counts Great Whites only. The optional rare-catch title/sound excludes Jawbus, preserving its separate local-arrival-only alert. The rare-creature world outlines use the same entity-name filter as the HP panel and remain visible-only (depth-tested). Legendary and Trophy Fish notifiers accept Fakepixel system-chat messages only. The Fakepixel notifier adaptations are attributed under LGPL-3.0-or-later in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md). The remaining Feesh-inspired quality-of-life features are attributed under Apache-2.0 there. The separate splash-to-pull stopwatch starts from a splash-named sound within 3.5 blocks of your bobber and freezes when its downward-position detector recognizes the pull. Wolf Slayer Power Orb timers start from a local right-click with the Wiki-listed durations: Radiant/Mana Flux 30s and Overflux/Plasmaflux 60s; the Totem timer additionally syncs to the local player's in-world remaining-time nameplate when visible. The Jawbus title accepts only the local arrival message; party and player chat messages are ignored. Reward, boss, and announcement markers are maintained in `SpiderDropAlert.java` and can be updated if Hypixel changes its messages or the wiki classifications.

## Build from source

This project uses the Forge 1.8.9 MDK and Gradle wrapper. RNG Carried Bald draws its settings window like the NotEnoughFakepixel one: a **Categories** tree on the left with expand/collapse arrows and a card per feature on the right, grouped as **Quality of Life** (Deployables, Quick Note, Appearance, Layout), **Dungeons** (Boss Hitboxes, Dragon Hitboxes, Dragon Areas), **Fishing** (Mob Counter, Catch Notifications, Sea Creature HP) and **Slayer** (Tracker, History), plus About. Dungeons carries the F7/M7 hitboxes and Quality of Life carries the deployables timers. The previously added Fishing Alert countdown and red `!!!` bite marker have been removed; Fakepixel-only Legendary Sea Creature and Trophy Fish notifiers remain in Catch Notifications. The full-screen editor is where HUD panel sizes are set, by dragging a box corner; independent translucent background-opacity controls remain, and HUD panels hide while chat is open or the player list is held. Existing rare-creature outlines/alerts, tracked-creature efficiency, Great White Festival personal best, fishing counters and splash-to-pull stopwatch are retained. Features that need newer Fabric APIs, data not reliably exposed to a 1.8.9 client, or counts of non-Legendary catches were not ported. See [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) for attribution and license. HUD drawing, F7/M7 rendering, message parsing, and fishing session statistics remain in focused classes. The Dungeon overlay recognizes normal `Catacombs - Floor VII/F7`, compact `Master Mode F7`, and Fakepixel's `dung-#` M7 scoreboard when a named dragon health line is present. See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) for the source layout. Build with Java 8 by running `bash gradlew clean test build` from this directory; the reobfuscated JAR is produced under `build/libs`.
